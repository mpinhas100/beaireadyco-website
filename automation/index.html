    Automation vs. an AI agent

    You'll usually want a mix. We'll recommend whichever is simplest for the job.

    Automation

    Follows the same steps every time. Fast, predictable, and cheap to run.

    • "When a form comes in, add it to the sheet and send a welcome email"
    • Best for clear rules and repeat tasks

    AI agent

    Reads, decides and takes the next step, checking with you when it matters.

    • "Read new inquiries, sort them, draft replies and flag the urgent ones"
    • Best when every case is a little different

    With one task your team repeats every week and doesn't enjoy. A quick win builds confidence before you automate anything bigger.

    That's why we test with you first and add approval steps for anything customer-facing or important. You stay in control, and we tune it based on what you see.

    The goal is to take the repetitive work off their plate so they can focus on customers, decisions and the work that needs a person.

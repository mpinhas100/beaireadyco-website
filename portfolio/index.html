Example screen with made-up companies. The real Tracker holds private client data, so it isn't shown here.

  1. 01Finds new prospects

    Every day it searches public business listings for companies that fit, and adds them as new contacts.

  2. 02Drafts the first email

    AI writes a short first email for each one. The owner approves the drafts before anything is sent.

  3. 03Sends within safe limits

    Approved emails go out a few at a time each day, so the account isn't flagged as bulk mail.

  4. 04Reads the replies

    Gmail is connected, so replies update each company's status on their own.

  5. 05Keeps you on top of it

    Follow-ups due, a daily summary and upcoming appointments, all in one place.

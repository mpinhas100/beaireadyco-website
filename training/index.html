Questions people ask

Our team has never used AI. Is that okay?

Yes. We start from the basics and move at your team's pace. Many of the best results come from people who were skeptical at first.

Which AI tools do you teach?

ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Google Gemini, with a focus on whichever ones your company already has or plans to use.

Is it online or in person?

Either. We can come to your office or run live sessions online, whichever works best for your team.

How do you keep our company data safe?

Safe use is part of every session. Your team learns what's okay to share with AI tools, what isn't, and how to use the privacy settings that protect your data.

Can you tailor it to our industry?

That's the point. We build the examples and practice tasks from your team's real work, so it applies right away.

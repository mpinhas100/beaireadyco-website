BeAIReadyCo School Privacy Notice

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Who we are

BeAIReadyCo runs live, project-based AI classes for kids ages 8 and up, in person as well as online, with families, schools, after-school programs, community centers, libraries and other organizations. You can reach us at beaireadyco@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Parent or guardian: name and email address.
  • Child: first name, last name or initial, nickname, and a student ID we assign.
  • Class information: which class and group the child is in. We take class attendance.
  • Learning activity: we work mostly in digital format, assigning projects and quizzes. At times, we give out pamphlets or books for additional activities.
  • Messages: you and/or your child will receive messages by email, phone, or the BeAIReadyCo app from your child's teacher or BeAIReadyCo admin/owner.

We do not ask for a child's birthday, address, phone number or photo without your permission.

Why we collect it

We use this information to run classes, show a child's progress to their parent, and send messages about classes. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

  • You, as the parent or guardian.
  • The teacher for your child's class.
  • BeAIReadyCo staff who run the program.
  • Your school or organization that we have partnered with.

Consent

We ask for your consent before we collect your child's information. You give consent by checking the box when you create your account. You can review, correct or delete your child's information at any time, and you can withdraw your consent by emailing beaireadyco@gmail.com.

Children and accounts

Children do not sign in with Google or any other outside account. Once you as the guardian create the family account in the app, you will be able to add your child by name, add an email address for them, and create a password. After which, the admin will approve the new family and child addition, and both you and your child will be able to log in.

How long we keep information

We keep a child's records while they are enrolled and delete them after a parent asks.

Keeping information safe

We limit access to people who need it for the class and protect accounts with sign-in. No system is perfect, so please tell us right away if you think your account has been used without your permission.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date above.

Our curriculum

Every program follows the same five-step path, adjusted for each age group.

  1. AI fundamentals

    What AI is, how it learns, what it's good at and where it makes mistakes.

  2. Prompt engineering mastery

    Asking clear questions and guiding AI to useful, accurate answers.

  3. Practical tools & applications

    Hands-on with today's AI tools for writing, research, images and more.

  4. Creative problem solving

    Using AI to brainstorm, plan and solve problems while thinking for yourself.

  5. Real-world projects

    Finished projects students can show, from stories and apps to presentations.

AI School questions

What ages does BeAIReadyCo AI School teach?

We teach kids from age 8, teens, adults and parents. We also run AI training for business teams. Each group gets lessons matched to its age and goals.

Does my child need to know how to code?

No. Our AI classes start from zero. Students learn how AI works, how to ask it good questions and how to check its answers, then build up to creative and real-world projects.

Are classes in person or online?

Both. BeAIReadyCo is based in Queens, New York, and we also teach online, so students across New York City and beyond can join.

Do you offer private lessons or group classes?

Yes, both. Choose small-group classes or one-on-one private lessons. We also run AI workshops for schools, camps, libraries and parent groups.

How much do AI classes cost?

It depends on the program, format and number of sessions. Book a free info call and we'll recommend the right class and share current schedules and pricing.

How do I enroll?

Click Enroll today to book a free info call. We'll talk about the student's age, experience and goals, then help you pick a class and a start date.

Is AI safe for kids to use?

With guidance, yes. Every program teaches safe, responsible AI use: protecting personal information, spotting mistakes and made-up answers, and using AI to learn rather than to cheat.

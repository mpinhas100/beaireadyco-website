Creative Studio questions

What is the AI Creative Studio?

It's BeAIReadyCo's creative arm. We help musicians, creators and brands use AI for songwriting, music production, audio content and creative direction, always with a human creative in charge.

Do I need music or tech experience?

No. Bring your idea, a voice memo, lyrics or just a feeling. We guide you through the process and explain every step.

Can AI write a whole song for me?

AI can help with lyrics, melodies, beats and arrangements, but the best results come from combining it with your ideas and taste. We use AI to speed up and expand your creativity, not replace it.

Do you help with podcasts and video content?

Yes. We help podcasters and content creators with audio content, intros, sound design, scripts and creative direction for their channels.

Can we work together online?

Yes. We work with creators in Queens and across New York City in person, and with creators anywhere online.

Can you teach me to use AI music tools myself?

Yes. Along with done-for-you projects, we offer one-on-one coaching so you can learn the tools and create on your own.

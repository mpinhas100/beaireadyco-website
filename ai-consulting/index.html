AI consulting questions

What does an AI consultant do for a small business?

We look at how your business runs today, find the tasks that eat up your time or lose you customers, and show you exactly where AI can help. Then we give you a clear plan, recommend the right tools, and can set them up and train your team.

How much does AI consulting cost?

Your first 30-minute consultation is free. After that we give you a fixed quote based on the size of your business and what you need, so there are no surprise fees. Many clients start with a single AI Opportunity Audit.

Do I need technical skills or a big budget?

No. We work with small businesses and explain everything in plain language. Most of the tools we recommend are affordable, and we focus first on quick wins that pay for themselves.

Which AI tools do you recommend?

We're tool-neutral. Depending on your needs we may recommend ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, an AI receptionist, or automation tools like Zapier, and we pick what fits your budget and the software you already use.

Is it safe to use AI with my customer data?

It can be when it's set up correctly. Every plan includes guidance on privacy, which data should never go into AI tools, and a simple AI use policy for your team.

Do you work with businesses outside Queens?

Yes. We're based in Queens and work with businesses across New York City in person, and anywhere online.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients get at least one quick win in the first few weeks, such as faster replies to leads, less time on admin, or more consistent marketing. The 30-60-90 day plan maps out the rest.
